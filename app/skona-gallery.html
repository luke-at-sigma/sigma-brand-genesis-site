Print view

Every ad below is soft-proofed: converted to CMYK and back, so you see roughly how it prints on a GRACoL 2013 coated press.
Sigma blue#1A70F1 → C90 M60 Y0 K0
Bric blue#7FB5FF → C44 M20 Y0 K0
Lime#E1FA72 → C15 M0 Y69 K0
Grid#E7EEF9 → C11 M3 Y0 K0
Section gray#F7F7F8 → C0 M0 Y0 K4
Black#000000 → C60 M40 Y40 K100

Guides on every ad follow OUTFRONT’s NYC Brand Train spec: the shaded edge is hidden by the car frame, the pink line is the live (visible) area, and the blue dashed line is the safety area that type, logos and QR codes stay inside. Left half of each chip is the screen colour, right half the print simulation. Brand colours use SKONA’s builds from their approved files: Sigma blue C90 M60 Y0 K0, bric C44 M20 Y0 K0, lime C15 M0 Y69 K0, and rich black C60 M40 Y40 K100 for type and the logo. #1A70F1 itself sits outside what CMYK inks reach, so print reads deeper and less electric than the screen. Everything else converts through GRACoL 2013 (CRPC6), the US coated-press reference, and greys print in black ink only. The print files are vector PDF/X-4s on the same builds, layered like SKONA’s (bg, artwork, copy, guides) and named their way; if the printer sends their own profile, they rebuild against it.
